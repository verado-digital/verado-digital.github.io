Privacy & Cookie Policy.

How Verado Digital collects, uses, and protects your information.

Last updated: August 2026

Who We Are

Verado Digital ("we", "us", "our") is operated by Omotoke Ojo. This policy explains what information we collect through veradodigital.com and how it's used. If you have questions, contact us at ojoesther6270@gmail.com.

Information We Collect

  • Contact form submissions — name, email, phone, and project details you submit through our Jotform contact form.
  • Newsletter / audit requests — name, email, and store URL submitted through our popup form.
  • Live chat messages — conversations you have with us through our Tawk.to chat widget.
  • Usage data — general information about how you browse the site, collected automatically through cookies and similar technologies.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to inquiries and provide requested services or audits
  • To send occasional updates, if you've opted in through our newsletter form
  • To improve our website's design, content, and performance
  • To communicate with you via email, phone, or WhatsApp about your project

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Cookies

Cookies are small files stored on your device that help websites function and remember your preferences. We use the following types:

  • Essential cookies — required for basic site functionality, such as remembering that you've dismissed a popup or cookie banner.
  • Functional cookies — used by embedded tools like our live chat widget (Tawk.to) to maintain your conversation session.

You can accept or decline non-essential cookies via the banner shown on your first visit. You can also clear cookies at any time through your browser settings.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services, each with their own privacy practices:

  • Jotform — processes and stores contact form submissions. See Jotform's Privacy Policy.
  • Tawk.to — powers our live chat widget. See Tawk.to's Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts this website and may log basic technical/server data (such as IP address) as part of standard web hosting.
  • WhatsApp — used for direct messaging if you choose to contact us that way, governed by WhatsApp's own privacy policy.

Data Retention

We retain contact form and audit request submissions for as long as needed to respond to your inquiry and maintain business records, or until you ask us to delete them.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by emailing ojoesther6270@gmail.com. You may also unsubscribe from any email communications using the link provided in those emails, or by contacting us directly.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision.
