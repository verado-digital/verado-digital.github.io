The Complete SEO Checklist Every Ecommerce Store Owner Needs

E
Esther Ojo
Founder, Verado Digital
January 26, 2026 7 min read

Paid ads stop working the moment you stop paying. SEO compounds — every product page and blog post you optimize keeps working for you months and years later. Here's the checklist that actually moves the needle for ecommerce stores.

Technical foundations

  • Every page loads in under 3 seconds, especially on mobile
  • Images are compressed and use descriptive alt text
  • The site has a clean, submitted XML sitemap
  • URLs are short, readable, and keyword-relevant (no strings of numbers)
  • Duplicate product variants are handled with canonical tags

On-page optimization

Every product and collection page should target a specific keyword a real customer would type into Google — not just your brand name. Titles, meta descriptions, and H1 tags should all reflect that keyword naturally, without stuffing.

Content that earns rankings

Product descriptions written only for the algorithm read badly and rank badly. The stores that win combine genuine buyer-focused copy with the specific terms customers search for — think 'best running shoes for flat feet' rather than just 'running shoes'.

SEO isn't a one-time task you finish. It's an asset you keep building, page by page.

Link building and authority

  • Get listed in relevant directories and marketplaces
  • Reach out for guest posts or collaborations in your niche
  • Encourage customers to link to you from their own blogs or reviews
  • Build internal links between related products and blog content

Local and structured data

If you have any physical presence, claiming and optimizing your Google Business Profile helps significantly. Adding structured data (schema markup) for products — price, availability, reviews — also makes your listings stand out directly in search results.

Track it monthly, not once

SEO isn't 'set and forget.' Reviewing your top pages, fixing broken links, and refreshing older content every month is what separates stores that keep climbing from stores that plateau after the first win.
